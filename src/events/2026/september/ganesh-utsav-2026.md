# Ganesh Utsav 2026 (गणेश उत्सव)

Maharashtra Mandal Krakow invites the entire community to celebrate **Ganesh Utsav 2026** on **Sunday, 20 September 2026** at **Hotel Zakliki, Krakow**. Come together for a joyful day of puja, cultural performances, delicious food and devotion to Lord Ganesha.

## Event Details

- **Date:** Sunday, 20 September 2026
- **Venue:** Hotel Zakliki, Krakow
- **Organized by:** Maharashtra Mandal Krakow

## Program Outline

| Time | Program |
|------|---------|
| 10:30 – 11:30 | Sthapana / Puja Aarati |
| 11:30 – 12:30 | Cultural Program (Part 1) |
| 12:30 – 14:00 | Lunch |
| 14:00 – 15:30 | Cultural Program (Part 2) & Bhajan |
| 15:30 – 16:30 | Snacks |
| 16:30 – 17:00 | Visarjan |

## How You Can Participate

- **Perform:** We welcome participants for the cultural program — dance, singing, skits or instrumental performances. Please contact the MMK team to register your performance.
- **Volunteer:** Help with decorations, puja arrangements, food service and cleanup. Every pair of hands makes the celebration better.
- **Attend:** Bring your family and friends along for a day of devotion and fun. Traditional dress encouraged!

## Looking Forward

We look forward to celebrating Ganesh Utsav with the whole community. Mark your calendars and stay tuned for further updates on the venue and program details!
