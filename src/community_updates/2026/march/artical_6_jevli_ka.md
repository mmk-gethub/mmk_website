Jevli Ka? (जेवली का?) – "Have you eaten?" – this seemingly trivial question may be the quintessence of true love. As Vikrant Mahalle, an Indian actor, director, screenwriter, and producer based in Krakow, explains, his new musical project and music video are "a tribute to lovers and dreamers who find magic in the mundane. It's a song for a husband who texts his wife during a break from work, an elderly couple who smile at each other silently, teenagers falling in love for the first time. The question 'Jevli Ka?' fits into the rhythm of everyday married life. It emphasizes the beauty of ordinariness and the silent understanding that gives meaning to being together. It's the universal language of love..."

The director, producer, and main performer of the song, Vikrant Mahalle, has lived and worked in Krakow for years, promoting film, theater, and other initiatives promoting Polish-Indian integration through culture. The song "Jevli Ka?" It was composed with lyrics by Vikrant by Ketan Jain and produced by Voxaham Production and Cuttingfilms, in collaboration with Humming Tales, Tea4Theatre, BeHappy Museum, and the Multicultural Center in Krakow. The music video features Rashmi Gawande, Anved Mahalle, Advika Giri, and dancers from the Polish Kalaivani Dance Group.

The project was created thanks to the collaboration and immense commitment of individuals and entities from Poland, India, and the Netherlands, committed to building mutual cultural relations and fascinated by the traditions and contemporary aspects of India. This is the first music video produced in collaboration with the Multicultural Center. Be sure to listen and share this vibe with your friends!
