# Kids Act: Vishwa Marathi Ekankika 2026 (Session 3)

We are thrilled to share that the children of Maharashtra Mandal Krakow participated in the **Vishwa Marathi Ekankika (VME) 2026 Session 3**. This prestigious global one-act play competition provided our young performers with an incredible opportunity to showcase their talent in Marathi theater.

The kids put in weeks of practice, mastering their Marathi dialogues and perfecting their expressions. Their performance was a testament to their dedication and our community's commitment to keeping our language and theater traditions alive in the younger generation.
