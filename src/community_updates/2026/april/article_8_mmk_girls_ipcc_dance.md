# Dance Participation by MMK Girls in IPCC Event

Our talented MMK girls recently participated in the **IPCC (Inter-Cultural Partnership Center)** event in Krakow. It was a proud moment as they represented the Marathi community through a series of captivating dance performances.

The performance was a beautiful blend of traditional Maharashtrian folk and contemporary styles, showcasing the grace and energy of our culture. The audience was enthralled by the vibrant costumes and the synchronized choreography.

We are incredibly proud of our girls for their hard work and dedication in preparing for this performance. Such events provide a wonderful platform to share our heritage with the broader community in Krakow.
