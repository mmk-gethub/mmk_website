# Shivjayanti 2026

The birth anniversary of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj was celebrated with great enthusiasm and pride by Maharashtra Mandal Krakow on 21 February 2026 in Krakow, Poland. Members of the Marathi community in Krakow gathered in large numbers to participate in the celebration and make the event memorable.

The program began at 3:00 PM with a traditional puja ceremony, followed by a variety of cultural activities and interactive games. The event created a joyful environment where the Marathi community could come together, celebrate their heritage, and spend quality time with one another.

## Celebration Highlights

- The celebration started with a traditional puja ceremony dedicated to Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj.
- A series of cultural performances were presented by both children and adults, including songs that were warmly appreciated by the audience.
- One of the most special attractions of the event was the storytelling session by Abhijit Vaidya, specially organized for children.
- Through his engaging and expressive storytelling style, he beautifully narrated inspiring stories related to Shivaji Maharaj, which the children enjoyed immensely.
- The program also included interactive games and community activities, encouraging participation and interaction among attendees.
- Participants spent time socializing, sharing conversations, and enjoying the festive atmosphere together.
- The event was made successful through the efforts of dedicated volunteers including Amruta Bichkar, Anuradha Giri, Preeti Rane, Rekha Jadhav, Dipika Salgude, Akshay Bapat, Nikhil Jadhav, Abhijit Vaidya, Vijay Bichkar, Kaustubh Deshmane, and Ganesh Murudkar, who contributed significantly to the planning and execution of the program.

## Traditional Significance

- Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj Jayanti is celebrated to honor the birth and legacy of Chhatrapati Shivaji Maharaj, the founder of the Maratha Empire.
- Shivaji Maharaj is remembered for his courage, visionary leadership, and dedication to Swarajya (self-rule).
- His life and achievements continue to inspire generations with values of bravery, justice, leadership, and respect for all communities.
- Celebrating this occasion helps the Marathi community reconnect with their history, cultural pride, and shared identity.
- Even while living far from India, such celebrations help the Marathi community in Krakow pass on the inspiring legacy of Shivaji Maharaj to younger generations.
