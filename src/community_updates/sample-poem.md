# The Colors of Krakow

The old town square is gray and bright,  
Bathing in the morning light.  
But when we gather, dance, and sing,  
We give the ancient cobbles wings.

Our vibrant clothes, a joyous hue,  
A splash of saffron, green, and blue.  
Though miles away from where we start,  
We keep our homeland in our heart.

*Written with love by Aarushi.*
